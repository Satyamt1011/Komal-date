You asked me something

“Give me a reason
you love me.”

I thought about it.

And then I realised something…

I don't think I can give you just one. ❤️
Reason 01
👀

Your eyes.

If I'm being completely honest…

They were the first thing I fell in love with.

I don't know exactly what it was.

Maybe the way they look at me.
Maybe the way they change when you're happy.

But the eyes came first.

Reason 02
🌙

Your dreams.

The more I got to know you, the more I saw the person behind those eyes.

You have bigger dreams.

You want more from life.

You're ambitious, you have your own goals, and even when life gets exhausting, there's still that part of you that wants to keep moving forward.

I love that you don't dream small just because life sometimes makes things difficult.
Reason 03
🦋

Your independence.

You are your own person.

You have your own opinions, your own choices, your own way of doing things.

I don't love you because you need someone.

I love you because you're you.

You don't have to become different to be loved.

I love that there's a strong, independent woman behind all those little moments I adore.

Reason 04
🍜

Your little things.

Maybe you don't realise how much I notice them.

🍜 “Okayish” Maggi
🥘 Yummy bhindi-roti
🌙 Parking under the moon
🤏 Those cheek pulls

You might call them small.

I don't.

A five-minute meeting before going home can stay in my head longer than some big plans.

A tiny gesture from you can make an ordinary day feel special.

Reason 05
❤️

Your personality.

This is the part that came later.

I fell for your eyes first.

But I stayed for everything I discovered after that.

Your ambition.
Your independence.
Your stubborn little ways.
Your love for food.
Your love for the moon.
Your silly moments.
Your softness that sometimes appears when you least expect it.

The more I knew you, the more there was to love.

🌙
The answer

Why do I love you?

Because it wasn't one moment.

It wasn't one quality.

It wasn't even just your eyes.

I fell in love with your eyes first.

Then I met your personality.

Then I started noticing the little things.

And somewhere along the way…

you became someone whose happiness, dreams, little gestures and ordinary moments started mattering to me.

That's probably my real reason.

For you
🌙

You asked for a reason.

So here's the simplest answer I could give you.

I don't love some perfect version of you.

I love getting to know you.

Your eyes were where it started.

Your personality is what made me stay.

And the little pieces of you are what keep making me fall a little more.

🌷 🌸 🌹 🌷 🌸

You are special to me.

Not because I decided you should be.

But because, somehow, you became that person.

— Satyam ❤️
