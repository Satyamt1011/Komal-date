I wanted that hug because you once said you don't really feel anything by a hug… and I wanted you to feel what I couldn't put into words.

We hugged twice, but both times a random surrounding sound disturbed us.

The third time, our hug was complete.

It gave me an everlasting peace and comfort beyond words. It felt like home. Like I had achieved everything. Like I had felt loved after a very long time.

Our arms were around each other. It felt like a safe place to me — and I hope it felt like a safe place to you too.

And before I let you go… I rubbed my cheeks against yours. Just a tiny, silly attempt to make sure you felt something. ☺
